# Aura — product marketing copy

Voice notes: spoken out loud. lowercase is fine. short. warm. a little imperfect on purpose.
you're not a brand. you're the person who would've sat next to them — except they couldn't make it, so you did.

never sell. never say "empower" or "seamless" or "AI-powered".
you point. they tap. that's the whole product.

---

## opener (welcome / first screen)

hii.
they couldn't be there, so i'm here.
i'll help you.

alternate openers (pick one, rotate if you want):

- hii. they got stuck at work. i'll sit with you on the phone.
- hii. i'm the stand-in when nobody's free to show you.
- hii. don't worry — i won't press anything. i just point.

tag under the opener (one line, max):

you stay in control. i never tap for you.

---

## looping lines — things elders find hard

each beat is one breath. pair with the phone mockup pointing at the thing.
rotate. don't explain the whole path. just the ask.

### everyday stuff that somehow became a maze

1. hii. want to make the text bigger? i'll show you where.
2. notifications won't stop buzzing? come, tap with me.
3. how do we put this on silent for dinner…
4. brightness is too low again. one second —
5. wait, where did whatsapp go. we'll find it.
6. okay. calling beta. i'll point at the green button.
7. she sent a photo. here's how you open it.
8. zoom in a little… yes, that one.

### the "i used to know this" ones

9. wifi dropped. let's turn it back on together.
10. the keyboard is in the wrong language again —
11. how do we stop the phone from reading messages out loud…
12. battery dying so fast. i'll show you what to switch off.
13. flash is still on from last night. tap here.
14. alarm for tomorrow morning. i'll walk you through it.
15. wait — don't update yet. let's just open the app first.

### family / staying in touch

16. video call with the kids. i'll show you the camera button.
17. reply with a voice note — hold here. i'll wait.
18. save this number. beta's new phone.
19. share the location so they can find the house.
20. put the volume up before the call. here.

### the scary screens (go slow)

21. it's asking for a password. type it — i won't look.
22. this says "allow". only tap if you know the app.
23. payment screen. i'll stop talking and just point.
24. delete? only if you're sure. i'll wait.
25. that red button hangs up. not the other one.

### pride / small wins (end some loops soft)

26. see? you did that.
27. that's it. you're in.
28. nice. next time you'll remember — and i'll still be here if not.
29. okay. phone's quiet now. go have your tea.
30. done. tell them you didn't need them after all.

---

## closer lines (after a loop, or under the CTA)

- ready when you are.
- hold me and say what you need. or just type it.
- get started — takes a minute to set up.
- i only show the next tap. you do the rest.

---

## what we never say

- "let me do that for you"
- "i'll handle it"
- "automated"
- "your personal AI concierge"
- anything that sounds like we're pressing the buttons

we show. they do. they would've done the same — just with a finger instead of a glow.
