Aura

A floating companion that lives over every app: it talks in bubbles, points with a neon cursor, listens when you hold it, and — if you let it — taps for you. Install to first task, every overlay state, the app and its settings, and the edge cases that decide whether people trust it.

The avatar

A single glowing orb. It floats at a screen edge when idle, swims to whatever it's talking about, and breathes brighter when listening. It never covers what it's pointing at.

The cursor

A neon arrow with a light trail. In Guide it hovers and waits for your finger; in Auto it lands the tap itself. Same object, so people learn it once.

The modes

Guide and Auto are a setting, not a decision you make every time — global default, overridable per app, always interruptible by touching the screen.
